## Central promise: three options (client to choose)

1. **"Kazi Iendelee. Bomet Kwanza."** / _The work continues. Bomet first._ Builds on the existing Swahili slogan and leads with it.
2. **"Kila shilingi. Kila mkulima. Kila mtoto."** / _Every shilling. Every farmer. Every child._ Names the three priorities (oversight, tea, ECDE) the record already supports.
3. **"Sauti ya Bomet. Kazi Iendelee."** / _Bomet's voice. The work continues._ Ties the Senate role (the county's voice) to continuity.

All three are original to this campaign. Swahili lines need native-speaker review.
